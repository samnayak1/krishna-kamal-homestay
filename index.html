01 — The home

Built from the red earth around it.

02 — Udupi travel & tourism

Your stay in Udupi, close to it all.

Planning your travel to Udupi? Krishna Kamala Heritage Homestay in Marpalli offers comfortable accommodation in Udupi for families, pilgrims and holidaymakers — a quiet base for darshan at the Sri Krishna Temple, evenings at Malpe Beach and everything in between.

Sri Krishna Temple

The heart of Udupi: the Sri Krishna Matha founded by Sri Madhvacharya, where devotees have darshan of Lord Krishna through the Kanakana Kindi window. An easy drive from the homestay.

Malpe Beach

One of coastal Karnataka’s best-loved beaches, with wide golden sand, sunsets over the Arabian Sea and boat trips to St. Mary’s Island.

Udupi travel & tourism

Temples, beaches, Udupi’s famous vegetarian cuisine and the Manipal area are all within reach. Spend the day exploring, then come home to a calm, air-conditioned stay in Udupi.
